Admissions

Admissions open, all year round.

Students from Matric to Master's level can join — no prior experience required. Here's who it's for, what's provided, and exactly how to enrol.

Who can join

One requirement: the willingness to learn.

There's no entry test and no background in tech expected. Whether you've just finished Matric or you already hold a Master's degree, the classes start from the basics and build up to real, paid freelance work.

What's provided

Free 1-week trial

Sit in on real classes before you decide to enrol.

Laptops provided

No need to own a computer to train with us.

Practical training

Hands-on work with real platforms, not just slides.

Certificate on completion

Proof of training to show clients and employers.

Online classes

Join remotely from anywhere in Pakistan.

How it works

Five steps from walking in to getting certified.

  1. Start your free trial week

    Visit us in Ahmed Pur East or join online, and sit in on real classes before you commit to anything.

  2. Choose your course

    Pick the path that fits your goals — our trainers help you decide during the trial week if you're unsure.

  3. Apply online

    Fill out the short admission form. It takes a few minutes.

  4. Begin training

    Practical, hands-on classes with a laptop provided for the full duration of your course.

  5. Get certified

    Finish with a certificate and the working skills to start applying for real freelance jobs.
